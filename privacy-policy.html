Via Belli: Frontline Rush

Frontline Rush does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. The game does not send any information to Scoops Dev or to anyone else, and it does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

The game saves your progress (levels won, stars, coins and upgrades) and your settings (such as sound, music, haptics and Reduce Motion) in files inside the game's own storage on your device. We cannot see these files. Deleting the app deletes them.

Its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data. The only system functions it declares are ones the game engine uses for its own operation: file timestamps, the time since the device started and free disk space. Frontline Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
