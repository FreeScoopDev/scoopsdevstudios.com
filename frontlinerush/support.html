About the game

Frontline Rush is a one-thumb road shooter for iPhone. Drag to steer your squad up the road; it fires on its own. Pick the better gates, recruit specialists, dodge the marked hazards and beat the boss at the end of each level.

Contact support

Email joe@scoopsdevstudios.com and we will get back to you.

How do I play?

Tap Play, choose a level and tap Play on its briefing. Drag a finger left and right to steer. Walk through the gates you want and keep out of anything marked red. In Settings, How to play explains the rules, and Replay tips shows the first-time tips again.

How do I unlock more levels?

Each level unlocks when you win the one before it. The Long March opens once you win level 20.

How do stars and coins work?

A win earns one to three stars, depending on how much of your squad is still standing at the end. Every level pays coins, even a loss. Spend them in Upgrades on permanent upgrades for each class and on guns for your base.

What does it cost?

Frontline Rush is a one-time purchase on the App Store, and everything in the game is included. There are no in-app purchases, no ads and no subscriptions. The App Store shows the price in your currency.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Frontline Rush has no accounts and no cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Reset progress in Settings starts over; it asks twice first.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game never connects to the internet.

Is the game accessible?

Settings has a Reduce Motion switch, which starts on when your iPhone's own Reduce Motion is on, and switches for sound, music and haptics. Gates show good or bad by their shape and sign as well as their colour, and buttons have VoiceOver labels.

How do I report a bug?

Email us and include:

  • your device (for example, iPhone 15)
  • your iOS version
  • the level, what happened, and what you expected
