Frontline Rush

A one-thumb road shooter for iPhone. Drag to steer, grow your squad, and take on the boss at the end of the road.

See how it plays ↓ Support and FAQ

About the game

Lead the charge up the road. Pick the better gate, dodge the shells, grow your army and smash the boss at the end of every level. Levels are quick, it plays in portrait with one thumb, and it works offline.

No ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

How it plays

Grow your squad

Walk through gates to add soldiers and steer clear of the red ones. Every five riflemen join up into a veteran, and recruit gates add gunners, grenadiers, AT gunners and medics.

Dodge and outsmart

Red rings mean a shell is coming, a red line means a sniper, a red lane means a charge. Every enemy has a counter, so the squad you build decides the fight.

Cards and supply drops

Pick a card to power up your squad for the rest of the level, and level cards up from I to III. Supplies parachute in mid-battle: stand under the crate you want.

Stars, coins and upgrades

Earn up to three stars by keeping your squad alive. Every level pays coins, win or lose, for permanent upgrades and guns for your base.

The campaign

Fight through seven regions, from the Countryside to the Beach, the Desert, the City, the Snow, the Jungle and the final Bridge. Each brings its own enemies and hazards and ends at a big boss. Defend levels turn it around: the road stops and you hold your base, wave after wave.

After level 20 the Long March opens: one long run of new stages built from the campaign, with your squad and cards carried from stage to stage.

Made to play anywhere

One thumb

Portrait, quick levels, and tips the first time you meet each new enemy and hazard.

Your settings

Switches for sound, music and haptics. Reduce Motion is built in and follows your iPhone setting.

Private by design

No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no data collected. It never connects to the internet.
