Via Belli Privacy Policy

Via Belli: Battles of History · Scoops Dev LLC · Last updated October 22, 2026

The short version

Via Belli does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no third-party SDKs. Your progress stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not send any information to Scoops Dev or to anyone else we work with. We do not know who plays the game, what you do in it, or which device you use. The game does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (battles won, stars, general ranks, skills and upgrades) and your settings (such as sound, music, haptics and Reduce Motion) in files inside the game's own storage on your device. We cannot see these files. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

The only online service the game uses is Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the game uses it to sign you in, record achievements and post your total stars to a leaderboard. This is handled by Apple, and your use of it is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The game sends nothing to Scoops Dev.

Tracking

Via Belli does not track you. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data. The only system functions it declares are ones the game engine uses for its own operation: file timestamps, the time since the device started (to time events in the game) and free disk space (before saving files).

Children

Via Belli is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
