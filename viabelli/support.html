About the game

Via Belli is a tower defense game set in historical battles. You build towers along the enemy's road, command your generals and try to hold the line, earning stars as you win.

Contact support

Email joe@scoopsdevstudios.com and we will get back to you.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Via Belli has no accounts and no cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do achievements work?

Achievements and the stars leaderboard use Apple's Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings to see them. Read more in the privacy policy.

Is the game accessible?

Settings has a Reduce Motion switch, which starts on when iOS's own Reduce Motion is on. Buttons in the game have VoiceOver labels.

How do I report a bug?

Email us and include:

  • your device (for example, iPhone 15)
  • your iOS version
  • what happened, and what you expected

Privacy

Via Belli collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.
